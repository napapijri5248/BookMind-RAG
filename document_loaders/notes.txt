Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is an artificial intelligence technique that improves the accuracy of large language models by fetching facts from external data sources before writing an answer.

How RAG Works

Ingestion:
     Documents or company data are loaded into an external storage system, such as a vector database.
Retrieval: 
    When you ask a question, the system searches the database for text chunks that match your query.
Augmentation: 
    The system adds these retrieved facts to your original question to build a richer prompt.
Generation: 
    The language model reads the combined prompt and writes an informed, grounded response.

Why RAG is Useful

Reduces Hallucinations:
     Grounding the model in real documents stops it from making up false facts.
Saves Money: 
    It avoids the high cost of retraining or fine-tuning entire models with new data.
Adds Fresh Data: 
    It gives static models access to live or newly updated company files.
